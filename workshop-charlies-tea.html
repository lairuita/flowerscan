Workshop · Saturday, September 12

Ikebana Workshop

A beginner-friendly introduction

茶理宜世 Charlie's Tea (DT)
546 Yonge St, Toronto
$85 CAD
tax in, includes one medium drink
Date & time
Saturday, September 12
1:00 – 2:30 pm

Invited by CIWA (Canadian International Well-being Association), we're hosting an ikebana workshop for National Self-Care Awareness Month this September.

A beginner-friendly introduction to ikebana — no experience needed, just curiosity. We'll cover the core principles of line, negative space, and seasonal material, then spend most of our time building an arrangement. Groups stay small, so there's room to slow down and really look at what you're making.

What's included
  • One medium drink
  • Seasonal stems and foliage
  • Kenzan, vessel, and clippers to use during the workshop
  • Guided instruction throughout
  • Flowers to take home
Add-on

Kenzan (flower frog) available to purchase at a discounted rate of $25 each (usual $40).

*Workshop tickets are non-refundable. Please consider carefully before purchasing.
