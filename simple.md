## The short version

We built a complete website for a small business in Toronto — from the homepage to the checkout button to the email that lands in your customer's inbox the moment they pay.

## What's on the site

**Six pages, all connected:**

- A home page that introduces the brand and pulls visitors in
- A subscription page where customers can read about the offer and buy in one click
- A workshops page where customers can see upcoming dates and book a spot
- A gallery page to show the work
- An About page to tell the story
- A FAQ page so customers can find answers on their own

Every page has the same navigation, the same look, and links to the others naturally. It feels like a real brand — because it is one.

---

## How payments work

When a customer clicks "Subscribe" or "Reserve your spot," a payment window appears right on the page. They enter their card, pay, and they're done — no getting redirected to another website, no confusion.

The moment the payment goes through, three things happen automatically:

1. The customer receives a confirmation email with everything they need to know
2. Their name, email, and order details are added to a private spreadsheet you can check anytime
3. The customer is taken to a thank-you page

You don't have to do anything manually. It just happens.

---

## The email

The confirmation email is branded — it uses your colours, your name, your voice. It's not a generic receipt. It reads like a note from a real person.

For subscriptions: it tells them what's coming and when.
For workshops: it tells them the date, what to bring, and how to cancel if they need to.

---

## The spreadsheet

Every customer who buys gets logged in a Google Sheet that you own. You can see names, emails, what they ordered, and when. It's your customer list, automatically maintained.

No separate software. No monthly fee for a CRM. Just a spreadsheet you already know how to use.

---

## Where it lives

The website is hosted on Vercel — a professional hosting platform used by companies of all sizes. It's fast, reliable, and the site loads in under a second anywhere in the world.

Your custom domain (like yourname.ca) points to Vercel, and everything is covered by a security certificate (the padlock in the browser) automatically.

## What it cost to run

After the build, the ongoing costs for a site like this are close to zero:

| Service | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| Hosting (Vercel) | Free |
| Emails (Resend) | Free up to ~3,000/month |

There are four things that need to happen:

You don't need to understand any of the technical parts. That's what we're for.
