FAQ

Questions, answered.

Subscription
Ikebana Box is a bi-weekly flower subscription service based in Toronto. Every two weeks, you receive a curated box of fresh seasonal stems with an ikebana-inspired arrangement instruction card. The cadence is intentional — timed to the natural life of an arrangement.
The Starter Box ($65 CAD, one-time) is for first-time subscribers. It includes seasonal stems, a kenzan (metal pin frog), a ceramic vessel, and an ikebana-inspired arrangement instruction card — everything you need to begin.

The Ikebana Box ($55 CAD per delivery) is the recurring bi-weekly subscription. It includes seasonal stems, an instruction card, and a flower care guide. The kenzan and vessel carry over from your Starter Box.
Deliveries and pickups happen every second Saturday. Orders need at least 2 days to process — subscribe by Thursday and your first box arrives that Saturday. Subscribe on a Friday or Saturday and your first delivery will be the following cycle, two weeks later. You'll receive a delivery email a day or two before your box arrives, with details on what stems are included and a link to that cycle's arrangement guide.
A kenzan is a metal pin frog used in ikebana to anchor and position stems in a vessel. It comes in your Starter Box and, with proper care, lasts for years. No adhesives, no foam — just clean, intentional placement.
Currently, delivery is within Toronto only. Pickup is always available at no extra charge. If you're outside the delivery area, reach out to hello@flowerscan.ca and we'll see what's possible.
Everything is managed through your subscriber portal, powered by Helcim. You can pause, skip a delivery, or cancel at any time — no emails required, no questions asked. A link to your portal is included in every delivery email.
Boxes are designed to be left safely at your door for a few hours without harm to the stems. If you have specific delivery instructions or concerns, add a note during checkout or email hello@flowerscan.ca.
Workshops
Workshop locations are shared with registered participants via email after booking. Sessions are held in intimate studio spaces in Toronto. Exact address and transit directions are included in your confirmation email.
Nothing. All materials are provided — stems, vessel or kenzan, clippers, and a printed instruction guide. Wear clothes you don't mind getting a little wet. Light refreshments are included.
Cancellations made more than 72 hours before the workshop receive a full refund. Cancellations within 72 hours are non-refundable, but your spot can be transferred to another person. Email hello@flowerscan.ca to arrange.

No experience necessary — only curiosity. Workshops are designed for beginners and experienced arrangers alike. The principles of ikebana are introduced from the ground up, with time for hands-on practice at whatever pace feels right.
