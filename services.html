What we do
  • 01
    Brand activations +

    Pop-up floral bars, gifting stations, and in-store activations for product launches, seasonal campaigns, and retail moments. We bring the flowers; your customers take the memory home.

  • 02
    Corporate events +

    Table arrangements, reception florals, and event styling for corporate gatherings, dinners, and hospitality events. Coordinated to your brand palette and setting.

  • 03
    Installations +

    Larger-scale floral statements for retail windows, exhibition spaces, and branded environments. Designed to photograph well and hold meaning in person.

  • 04
    Corporate gifting +

    Custom bouquets and arrangements for client gifting, staff appreciation, and brand partnerships. Individually wrapped, branded on request.

  • 05
    Workshops +

    Private ikebana-inspired workshops for teams, brand events, and client experiences. Up to 12 guests. All materials provided — stems, vessels, tools. See public dates.
