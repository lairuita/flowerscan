Ikebana Box

A bi-weekly ritual.

Sogetsu ikebana teaches that arrangement belongs to everyone — no rules, only presence. Each box is an invitation to show up and see what you make.

How it works

Three steps.

01

We source

Seasonal stems are handpicked from Toronto flower markets, curated for ikebana-inspired composition — line, asymmetry, space.

02

We deliver

Your Ikebana Box arrives every two weeks, timed to the natural life of your last arrangement. Never too soon.

03

You arrange

Follow the included instruction card or video guide to compose your arrangement. No experience necessary.
