The ritual

Why bi-weekly?

Every two weeks, a new Ikebana Box arrives — not because the last arrangement failed, but because you watched it live. Timed to the natural life of your stems, each delivery marks the quiet end of one arrangement and the beginning of another. This is wabi-sabi. This is mono no aware.
