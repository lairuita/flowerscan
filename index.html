The ritual

Why bi-weekly?

Not because the last arrangement failed — because you watched it live. You noticed which stem held longest. You noticed how the space between them changed as the days passed. The next box arrives when there is something new to notice. Timed to the life of what came before.

Sogetsu ikebana teaches that arrangement has no rules, only the arranger and the material — and what becomes possible between them. This is that practice, brought home.
