About

I came to ikebana through a love of flowers and a restlessness I didn't yet have words for. What I found was a practice that asked nothing of me except to be present with what was in front of me — a stem, a vessel, a moment.

Every box is sourced by hand — stems chosen for how they want to be placed, not just how they look. Every instruction card is written for that cycle's specific material. Not a template. A conversation.

Ray  ·  Toronto, ON

Flowerscan

The name means two things at once. flower scan — the way arrangement, when you let it, scans your inner state and shows it back to you. And Flowers can — possibility, permission, the belief that beauty is not a specialist's domain.

Every arrangement is unrepeatable. Every stem will fade. That is not a flaw. That is precisely the point. You made something true for this moment. That is enough.
