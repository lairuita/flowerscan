Workshop · Saturday, July 11

Matcha & Flower

Davang Luna
163 Sterling Rd, Toronto
Date & time
Saturday, July 11
5:00 – 6:30 pm
Price
$90 CAD
per person, tax included

Ikebana meets matcha. We'll arrange seasonal stems together at Davang Luna, one of Toronto's favourite matcha spots — and each ticket includes one standard iced or hot matcha latte to enjoy during the session. No experience needed.

What's included
  • Seasonal stems and foliage
  • Kenzan, vessel, and clippers provided
  • Guided instruction throughout
  • Your arrangement to take home
  • One standard iced or hot matcha latte
Upgrade available

Specialty matcha lattes — like the strawberry matcha — are available for an additional fee, ordered directly at the counter.

Tickets are non-refundable. If you can no longer attend, email hello@flowerscan.ca and we'll do our best to help.
