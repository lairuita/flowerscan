Ikebana 101

Again Again

A beginner-friendly introduction to ikebana. No prior experience needed — just curiosity. We'll cover the core principles of line, negative space, and seasonal material, then spend most of the time building an arrangement you take home. Groups are kept small so there's room to slow down and actually look at what you're making.

What's included
  • Seasonal stems and foliage
  • Kenzan, vessel, and clippers provided
  • Guided instruction throughout
  • Flowers to take home
  • Kenzan (flower frog) available to purchase at a discounted rate of $25 each (usual price $40)
