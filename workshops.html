Workshops

Learn Ikebana-Inspired Arrangement.

Intimate, in-person workshops in Toronto. Maximum 8 people. All materials included. No experience necessary — only curiosity.

What to expect

Two hours. One arrangement.

01

The principles

An introduction to ikebana-inspired arrangement — wabi-sabi, line, negative space, and the role of silence in composition. No lecture. A conversation.

02

The practice

Hands-on arrangement in either Moribana or Nageire style (noted per session). All materials provided: stems, kenzan or vessel, clippers.

03

Take it home

Your arrangement leaves with you. Light refreshments are included. Two hours well spent.

Pricing

$85 CAD per person.

Includes all materials, stems, vessel or kenzan, and light refreshments.

Private group workshops available for up to 8 people. Contact us to inquire.
